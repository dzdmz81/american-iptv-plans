## What should you look for in an American IPTV service?

And finally, test the service on your own setup. Tivi Live supports Android, iPhone, Smart TV, Firestick, MAG Box, Windows, MacOS and popular IPTV apps, according to its FAQ. Your internet connection and device still matter, especially when you are watching high-resolution live streams.
